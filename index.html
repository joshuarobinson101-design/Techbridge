What is TechBridge?

TechBridge is a program run by Baselink Services Limited. It connects learning with real-world experience, so that people who are building tech skills get the chance to practise them on practical work.

Many learners finish courses and tutorials but still feel unsure when it is time to work on real tasks. TechBridge exists to close that gap.

What TechBridge Offers

  • Practical Internship

    A 30-Day Practical Internship focused on hands-on work rather than lectures alone.

  • Skill Tracks

    Programs in Data Analytics and Web Development.

  • Real-World Experience

    Exposure to the kind of tasks and workflows used in actual projects.

  • A Learning Community

    A WhatsApp community where members connect, share updates and learn from one another.

Who the Program Is For

TechBridge is designed for people who want to build practical tech skills, including:

  • Students and graduates looking for hands-on experience
  • Beginners who want a structured way to start in tech
  • Career starters preparing for their first tech role
  • Self-taught learners who want to apply what they have learned

Programs Currently Available

Our 30-Day Practical Internship is currently open in two tracks:

Data Analytics

Learn to work with data: collecting, cleaning, analysing and presenting information so it can support better decisions.

Web Development

Learn to design and build websites, working with the tools used to create pages people can actually visit and use.

Why Join TechBridge?

  • Learn by doing. The internship is built around practical work.
  • Short and focused. 30 days is enough to build real momentum without a long commitment.
  • Beginner-friendly. You do not need to be an expert to get started.
  • Build confidence. Practising on real tasks helps you feel ready for the next step.
  • Be part of a community. Learn alongside others who share your goals.

How to Join the TechBridge Community

  1. Fill out the application form. Complete the TechBridge Internship Application Form.
  2. Join the WhatsApp community. Connect with the team and other members through the TechBridge WhatsApp Community.
  3. Follow us. Stay updated on LinkedIn and Facebook.
